How Sparkie Runs

1. Check goal and minimum account size Sparkie blocks paper and Live Mirror below $5,000.
2. Open Replay Sparkie sends the first symbol, first interval, first algo, date window, and trade size into Replay.
3. Start Replay Replay runs the historical paper test. Nothing goes to the broker.
4. Review and decide If paper/replay results look acceptable, restart from the normal Bot page with Live Mirror intentionally enabled.
