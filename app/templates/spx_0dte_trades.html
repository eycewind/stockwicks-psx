ℹ️ SPX Signal Columns Explained

Quick guide for reading the Last 5 SPX Signal Runs table
Time ET
Time when that bot run happened in Eastern Time.
Action
Final decision for that run: trade, no trade, or skipped.
Trend
Direction the bot was leaning: UP, DOWN, or NEUTRAL.
Signal
Final approved trade signal: CALL, PUT, or NONE.
Edge
Market quality state: STRONG_TREND, WEAK_TREND, or CHOP.
Velocity
Recent /ES movement speed in points per minute.
VWAP
Whether price is above or below VWAP, plus VWAP slope.
5m Structure
Bigger-picture 5-minute trend structure, such as HH_HL or LH_LL.
Reason
Exact reason the bot traded, blocked, or skipped the run.
